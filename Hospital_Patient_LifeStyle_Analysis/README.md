# 🏥 Hospital Patient Lifestyle Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a hospital patient lifestyle dataset to understand how different demographic, lifestyle, and medical factors are associated with heart attack risk.

The analysis was carried out using Python and various data analysis libraries to uncover patterns, identify relationships, and generate insights that could support healthcare organizations in understanding patient health trends.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Healthcare organizations are witnessing an increase in heart-related health issues. Understanding how lifestyle habits, demographic characteristics, and medical conditions relate to heart attack risk can help identify patterns that support preventive healthcare strategies.

This project aims to analyze patient lifestyle and medical information to explore factors associated with heart attack risk.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand the structure of the dataset.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Perform Univariate Analysis.
- Perform Bivariate Analysis.
- Analyze relationships between numerical variables using correlation analysis.
- Identify patterns associated with heart attack risk.
- Provide insights and recommendations based on the analysis.

## 📈 Key Findings

- Most numerical variables showed weak correlations.
- Cholesterol and BMI displayed only slight differences between heart attack risk groups.
- Lifestyle variables such as smoking and diet did not show strong visual separation in this dataset.
- Age distribution was similar across both heart attack risk groups.
- Heart attack risk appears to be influenced by multiple factors rather than a single variable.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- The dataset shows limited variation between heart attack risk groups.
- Most variables have weak correlations.
- This project identifies associations and does not establish cause-and-effect relationships.
- Results depend on the quality and characteristics of the dataset.
